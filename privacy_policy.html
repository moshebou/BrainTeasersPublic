Privacy Policy for Brain Teasers

Your privacy is important to us. It is Intellect Dynamics's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you through our app, Brain Teasers.

We only ask for personal information when we truly need it to provide a service to you. We collect it by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent. We also let you know why we’re collecting it and how it will be used.

Information We Collect

Our app may collect the following types of information:

  • Log Data: We may log information about your device and usage of our app, including your device's IP address, device type, operating system version, the time and date of your use, and other statistics. This data is collected to help us diagnose problems and improve our app.
  • Game Progress: We may save your game progress locally on your device to allow you to continue playing across sessions.
  • Analytics: The app uses Google Firebase services, which may collect data for analytics and to ensure service functionality. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our app
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our app
  • Understand and analyze how you use our app
  • Develop new features and functionality

Children's Privacy

Our application is designed to be accessible to a wide audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If as a parent or guardian, you believe that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately so we can take steps to remove this information.
